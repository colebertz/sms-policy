Operational System Alerts

Terms of Service

Effective Date: October 2026

This messaging service delivers automated operational system monitoring, scheduled task notifications, and urgent incident alerts to authorized system administrators.

Program Details

  • Message Frequency: Message frequency varies based on server activity, scheduled jobs, and system operational health.
  • Message and Data Rates: Message and data rates may apply for any messages sent to you from us and to us from you. Check with your wireless carrier for details.
  • Carriers Disclaimer: Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opt-Out and Support

  • Opt-Out: You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Simply text STOP to the sending number. After texting STOP, an SMS confirmation will be sent and no further messages will be delivered.
  • Support: If you experience issues or have questions, text HELP to the sending number or contact technical support at admin@yourdomain.com.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect mobile phone numbers provided directly by authorized system administrators solely to deliver automated transactional system alerts, operational health status, and administrative incident notices.

2. Mobile Information Non-Sharing Clause
No mobile information or SMS opt-in consent data will be shared, sold, rented, or leased to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes under any circumstances. Personal phone numbers collected for system alerting are used strictly for internal operational communication.

3. Data Security and Opt-Out

We implement technical and physical controls to secure collected contact data. Administrators can revoke consent and opt out at any time by replying STOP to any received message.
